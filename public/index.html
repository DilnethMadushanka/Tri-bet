How to play: place a bet before the round starts. The multiplier climbs from 1.00x. Press Cash out before it crashes to win your bet × the multiplier. If it crashes first, the bet is lost. The house edge is 3%, so on average you get back 97% of what you bet. Over time, the house wins.

Provably fair

  1. Before each round, the server picks a secret random seed and decides the crash point from it.
  2. It shows you the hash of the seed (SHA-256) before anyone bets. The hash locks in the result, but you can't work out the result from it.
  3. After the crash, the seed is revealed. Check that sha256(seed) matches the hash you saw, and that the seed gives the same crash point.

Crash point formula: h = HMAC_SHA256(key = seed, "betdouble"), x = first 13 hex digits of h / 2^52, crash = max(1, floor((100 − edge) / (1 − x)) / 100).
